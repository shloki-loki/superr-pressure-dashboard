How it works

Before the ball, estimate how possible outcomes could change the batting side’s win chance. After the ball, compare what actually happened with a peer baseline. All eligible delivery values enter the averages. Balls with a larger possible swing have more influence on the pressure average. Match weights are 1.00 for regular games, 1.25 for knockouts and 1.50 for finals.

The delta is pressure-weighted form minus overall form. The selection score combines the two averages. Match-removal ranges hold the fitted models fixed.
